Common questions

Straight answers.

How much does EdgeBreak cost?

EdgeBreak Standard is $19.99 USD per month and AI Unlimited is $29.99 USD per month after the 7-day free trial.

What is included in both plans?

Both plans include automated NASDAQ scanning, Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad scanners, AI Company Research, AI Chart Analysis, Guided AI, watchlists, My Workspace, TradingView charts and EdgeBreak Academy.

What is the difference between Standard and AI Unlimited?

Standard includes 15 full conversational AI questions each month. AI Unlimited removes that monthly conversational AI question limit. The rest of the core EdgeBreak platform remains included in both plans.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New eligible members receive a 7-day free trial before monthly billing begins.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. EdgeBreak subscriptions can be cancelled at any time and there are no lock-in contracts.

Does EdgeBreak tell me what to buy?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, research tools, market information and educational resources. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations.
