Why the scanner workspace exists

Researching every NASDAQ-listed stock manually would mean reviewing thousands of charts each day.

EdgeBreak is designed to reduce that workload. The platform combines automated scanning, manual technical scanners, charting, market activity context, AI-assisted research and personal watchlists so members can move from discovery to deeper investigation without starting from scratch every time.

The scanner does not replace analysis. It narrows the research universe so attention can be focused where the evidence is strongest.

EdgeBreak is designed to answer the first research question: “Where should I look?” The member still decides what deserves deeper investigation.

The EdgeBreak scanner ecosystem

Each scanner serves a different research purpose. Rather than treating every result as the same type of opportunity, EdgeBreak separates the market into stages of structure and development.

Automated Daily Scan

AI-assisted daily shortlist

The daily process scans the NASDAQ, applies EdgeBreak's rules-based filters and research criteria, then surfaces a focused group of stocks for deeper investigation. AI is used to assist the research layer rather than to generate buy or sell decisions.

Breakout Scanner

Stocks already through resistance

Use the Breakout Scanner to research stocks that have already moved beyond a defined resistance area according to the platform's rules-based model.

Pre-Breakout Scanner

Structures approaching resistance

Use the Pre-Breakout Scanner to study stocks with repeated resistance tests and improving lows before a confirmed move beyond the resistance zone.

Launch Pad Scanner

Longer developing bases

Launch Pad focuses on stocks building established trading ranges and technical bases, helping members study structures that may still be earlier in their development.

The Automated Daily Scan

The Automated Daily Scan is designed for members who want EdgeBreak to do the first stage of market narrowing for them.

Instead of manually testing every combination of filters, the daily process reviews the NASDAQ, removes weak or less useful structures and prioritises a small research list using technical evidence, unusual market activity and additional research checks.

The result is a starting point for investigation — not a list of recommendations.

The Breakout Scanner

The Breakout Scanner focuses on stocks that have already moved beyond an identified resistance level.

This is useful when researching the quality of the move itself: how the base formed, whether higher lows were present, how volume behaved and whether price remains close enough to the original structure to still be meaningful for early breakout research.

The Pre-Breakout Scanner

The Pre-Breakout Scanner is designed for structures that have not yet moved beyond resistance.

It looks for developing characteristics such as repeated resistance tests and consecutive higher lows so members can study the build-up before a possible breakout rather than only after price has already moved.

This makes it particularly useful for building watchlists and revisiting structures as they evolve.

The Launch Pad Scanner

Launch Pad looks for broader technical bases and established trading ranges.

These structures can spend weeks or months developing. Launch Pad is useful when the goal is to find earlier-stage candidates with support, resistance and a more defined base before price approaches the breakout stage.

Understanding scanner filters

Filters allow you to narrow the research list using specific characteristics.

Depending on the scanner, useful filters can include price group, resistance touches, higher lows, volume-related measures, lookback periods and other structural criteria.

The goal is not to find one magical filter setting. It is to use consistent combinations that answer a particular research question.

Resistance touches, higher lows and volume

Repeated resistance tests can show that price has returned to the same area several times. Higher lows can show improving structure beneath resistance. Volume adds context about market participation.

Each measure is useful on its own, but the strongest research comes from comparing several of them together rather than treating any one field as a signal.

Resistance Where has price repeatedly met selling pressure?
Higher lows Are pullbacks being supported at progressively higher prices?
Volume Is trading participation changing as the structure develops?

Drag-and-drop charting

The fastest way to understand scanner results is to move directly from the list into the chart.

EdgeBreak's chart workspace is designed so stocks can be moved into the chart area quickly for comparison, allowing members to cycle through candidates without repeatedly searching for each ticker.

This makes it easier to compare resistance, higher lows, volume and the broader weekly trend across several stocks in one research session.

AI Research buttons

Once a stock has been identified, the scanner provides a direct path into deeper AI-assisted research.

These buttons are designed to add context quickly without turning the platform into an advice engine.

Two AI research paths.

Use the stock context already in front of you and move directly into the type of analysis you need.

AI Company Research Summarises company overview, financial highlights, recent news, institutional activity context and relevant filings to support further investigation.
AI Chart Analysis Describes visible technical structure, trend, resistance, support and volume behaviour without providing buy, sell or hold instructions.

Build your personal watchlist

If a stock deserves continued attention, save it to My Workspace.

Your personal watchlist becomes a focused collection of research candidates that can be reopened, compared and monitored as the market structure changes.

This is especially useful for Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad candidates because the structure may take time to develop.

A complete scanner workflow

The individual tools become most useful when they are used as one consistent workflow.

Lesson summary

EdgeBreak combines automated discovery, manual scanners, interactive charts, AI-assisted research and personal watchlists into one research workflow. Each scanner serves a different purpose, while charting and AI tools help members investigate the strongest candidates in greater detail.

Key takeaways

  • The Automated Daily Scan provides a focused research shortlist.
  • Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad represent different stages of technical structure.
  • Filters help narrow research but should not be treated as signals.
  • Drag-and-drop charting speeds up comparison.
  • AI Research buttons add context without providing investment advice.
  • My Workspace turns scanner results into a personal ongoing research list.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Treating scanner results as recommendations.
  • Using one filter in isolation.
  • Skipping the chart review.
  • Assuming AI research can predict future price movement.
  • Saving too many stocks without a clear research reason.
