Welcome

Thank you for becoming an EdgeBreak member.

Whether you're completely new to breakout research or already have market experience, the goal is to help you develop a structured, repeatable approach to researching NASDAQ-listed stocks.

Rather than relying on opinions, predictions or market hype, EdgeBreak focuses on objective characteristics that can be researched consistently over time. These include market structure, resistance, higher lows, volume behaviour, unusual market activity and developing breakout structures.

The EdgeBreak philosophy

Every investor approaches the market differently, but useful research often follows a consistent process. Instead of searching for the next “hot stock”, EdgeBreak encourages members to study how quality setups develop before significant price movement occurs.

Strong research is built on patience, discipline and observation. Many interesting structures spend weeks or months developing before they attract wider market attention. Learning to recognise those characteristics takes practice, and that is exactly what the Academy is designed to teach.

EdgeBreak is designed to narrow the market, organise the evidence and give you a clearer place to begin your own research.

Research, not predictions

One of the most important principles throughout the Academy is that no indicator, chart pattern, scanner or AI system can predict future price movements with certainty.

Instead, EdgeBreak helps organise information so you can investigate stocks displaying characteristics that market participants often monitor during the development of potential breakout opportunities.

Every stock remains unique, and every investment decision should be based on your own independent research and circumstances.

How EdgeBreak works

EdgeBreak combines education with practical research tools.

Throughout the Academy, you'll learn the concepts behind breakout research. At the same time, you'll learn how to apply those concepts using the Automated Daily Scan, NASDAQ Scanner, Breakout and Pre-Breakout scanners, Launch Pad, AI research tools, TradingView charts and My Workspace.

The goal isn't simply to teach technical concepts. It is to help you build a repeatable research process that you can refine over time.

Your learning journey

The Academy has been designed as a step-by-step course. Each lesson builds on the previous one, so we recommend completing them in order.

You'll begin with the foundations of price action and market structure before progressing to more advanced topics such as breakout quality, volume behaviour, off-exchange market activity, multi-timeframe analysis and using the EdgeBreak platform effectively.

Finally, you'll apply what you've learned by studying real NASDAQ market examples and complete research walkthroughs.

Success comes from consistency

The purpose of EdgeBreak isn't to encourage more trading. It is to encourage better research.

Building watchlists, studying charts and following a structured routine can help create stronger research habits than constantly searching for something new every day.

The more consistently you apply the concepts taught throughout the Academy, the more familiar you will become with the structures and evidence EdgeBreak is designed to surface.

Lesson summary

EdgeBreak combines structured education with NASDAQ research tools designed to help members study potential opportunities through a consistent and repeatable process. Throughout the Academy, you'll learn how market structure develops, how to organise your research and how to use the platform to support your own independent analysis.

Key takeaways

  • EdgeBreak is a research and education platform.
  • No scanner, indicator or AI system can predict future market movements.
  • Consistency is more valuable than constantly chasing new opportunities.
  • The Academy teaches a structured research process from beginner foundations to more advanced concepts.
  • EdgeBreak tools are designed to support — not replace — your own research.
