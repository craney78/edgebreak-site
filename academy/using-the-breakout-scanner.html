What is the Breakout Scanner?

The Breakout Scanner searches for NASDAQ-listed stocks that have already moved beyond an established resistance area according to the EdgeBreak breakout model.

Unlike the Pre-Breakout Scanner, which focuses on developing structures beneath resistance, the Breakout Scanner is designed for studying what happens once price has moved through that level.

Every result is a research candidate. It is not a recommendation and it does not mean the move will continue.

A breakout result tells you that price has moved through resistance. The chart tells you whether the structure behind that move deserves deeper research.

What to compare first

Not every breakout develops from the same type of base. A stronger research process looks beyond the fact that price crossed resistance and asks how the move developed.

Structure How did price reach resistance? Repeated tests, higher lows and an orderly base can provide useful context.
Participation What happened to volume? Compare trading activity before, during and after the move through resistance.
Extension How far has price moved? A fresh breakout near its base is a different research situation from a stock already well above resistance.

Understanding the scanner metrics

The Breakout Scanner includes measurements that help compare the structure behind each result.

  • Current Price — where the stock is trading now.
  • Resistance Touches — how often price tested the identified resistance area.
  • Higher Lows — whether pullbacks improved beneath resistance.
  • Volume Ratio — recent trading activity compared with normal activity.
  • Distance Above Resistance — how far price has moved beyond the original breakout area.

These values are most useful when interpreted together rather than used as independent signals.

Fresh breakouts vs extended moves

A stock may continue moving well above its original resistance area, but the further it travels from the base, the more different the research context becomes.

EdgeBreak is designed to focus attention on structure, not simply on stocks that have already made the largest percentage move.

When reviewing a result, ask whether price is still close enough to the original breakout area for the base to remain useful context.

Resistance touches and higher lows

Repeated resistance tests show that price has returned to the same area multiple times. Higher lows show that pullbacks may be occurring at progressively higher prices.

When these characteristics appear together, they can describe a more organised build-up beneath resistance.

They still do not guarantee breakout continuation, so they should always be compared with volume, trend and broader market context.

Volume behaviour

Volume provides another layer of context.

Compare participation during the base, the move through resistance and the sessions that follow. A change in volume can help explain how much market participation was present, but it does not prove future direction.

Move straight into the chart

Scanner metrics are useful for narrowing the list, but the chart remains essential.

EdgeBreak lets you quickly load or drag a breakout candidate into the integrated TradingView chart so several results can be compared without repeatedly searching for tickers.

Review:

  • The broader weekly trend.
  • The original resistance area.
  • The structure of the base.
  • Higher lows.
  • Volume behaviour.
  • Nearby historical resistance.
  • Whether the breakout remains close to the original structure.

Use the AI Research buttons

Once the chart deserves further investigation, the AI Research buttons can add another layer of context.

From technical breakout to deeper research.

Use the breakout result as the starting point, then choose the type of context you need.

AI Company Research Review company overview, financial highlights, recent news, institutional activity context and relevant filings.
AI Chart Analysis Review a descriptive analysis of trend, resistance, support, volume and visible technical structure without buy, sell or hold recommendations.

Review what happens after the breakout

Breakout structures continue evolving after they appear in the scanner.

Some stocks remain above the breakout area, some consolidate, and some move back below resistance. The purpose of review is to study how the structure changes rather than assume one outcome is guaranteed.

This is where ongoing observation becomes valuable.

Save the strongest research candidates

If a breakout deserves continued attention, save it to My Workspace.

Your watchlist allows you to revisit the same candidate over days or weeks, compare new price behaviour with the original breakout and continue company research as new information appears.

A focused watchlist is generally more useful than saving every breakout result.

A complete breakout research flow

Filter → chart → investigate → revisit.

01 Filter Review active breakout results and narrow the list using structure and volume metrics.
02 Chart Load or drag candidates into TradingView and examine the full base and breakout.
03 Investigate Use AI Company Research and AI Chart Analysis for additional context.
04 Revisit Save the strongest candidates to My Workspace and monitor how the structure develops.
Practical exercise

Compare five active breakouts.

Open the Breakout Scanner and work through a small group of results.

  • Choose five breakout candidates.
  • Compare resistance touches, higher lows and volume.
  • Drag each into the chart and review the weekly and daily structure.
  • Identify which candidates remain closest to their original base.
  • Use AI Company Research on one candidate.
  • Use AI Chart Analysis on one candidate.
  • Save your strongest two research candidates to My Workspace.

Lesson summary

The Breakout Scanner focuses on NASDAQ stocks that have already moved beyond resistance. The best research comes from combining scanner metrics with direct chart review, company research, AI-assisted context and ongoing watchlist monitoring.

Key takeaways

  • A breakout result is a research candidate, not a recommendation.
  • Compare resistance touches, higher lows, volume and distance above resistance.
  • Fresh and extended breakouts represent different research situations.
  • Always inspect the chart behind the scanner result.
  • AI Research buttons add context after the technical structure has been reviewed.
  • Use My Workspace to monitor the strongest candidates over time.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Assuming every breakout is equally strong.
  • Focusing only on percentage movement.
  • Ignoring the structure before the breakout.
  • Skipping weekly-chart context.
  • Treating scanner ranking or AI analysis as a prediction.

Research reminder

The Breakout Scanner identifies stocks that meet EdgeBreak's rules-based breakout criteria for research purposes. Scanner results, charts and AI-assisted research do not represent financial advice, investment recommendations, trade signals or guarantees of future market performance.
