EdgeBreak has evolved into a research pipeline

The earlier version of this lesson relied on a much more manual sequence of scanning, filtering and chart review.

That is no longer how the platform works.

EdgeBreak now has a much stronger automated layer. The system scans more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks, applies multiple technical and market-activity checks, researches the strongest surviving candidates and surfaces a focused top-six shortlist for members to investigate further.

The modern workflow is no longer “search everything yourself.” EdgeBreak does the first stage of narrowing and research so you can spend more time investigating the strongest evidence.
Step 1 · Start here

Review the Automated Daily Scan.

The Automated Daily Scan is the fastest way to begin a research session.

Behind the scenes, EdgeBreak narrows the NASDAQ using rules-based technical filtering, market structure, volume behaviour, unusual off-exchange activity, larger purchase activity appearing across multiple markets and other research checks.

The final result is a small group of stocks selected for deeper investigation. They are research priorities, not recommendations.

The top six are already researched

The strongest candidates are not surfaced from technical structure alone.

Once the automated filtering and ranking process has narrowed the field, EdgeBreak adds company-level research so the final shortlist can be reviewed with more context already attached.

This helps members move beyond a ticker and chart into questions such as what the company does, whether revenue and earnings are improving, what recent catalysts exist and what risks may deserve attention.

Automated research before you even open the stock.

The top-six workflow combines multiple layers of evidence before the final shortlist is surfaced.

Technical structure Trend, resistance, higher lows, base quality, volume and how far price has moved from the underlying structure.
Market activity Unusual off-exchange activity and larger purchase activity appearing across multiple markets add supporting context.
Company research Financial direction, catalysts, recent developments, ownership context and balance-sheet or dilution risks can be investigated.
Research priority The strongest combined evidence helps determine which stocks deserve the most attention in the final top-six research list.
Step 2 · Use the manual scanners

Explore beyond the daily shortlist.

The Automated Daily Scan is one starting point, but EdgeBreak also gives you direct access to the scanner system.

Manual scanners are useful when you want to research a particular stage of market structure or compare more candidates than the daily top six.

NASDAQ Scanner Build your own research list Use structured filters to explore the wider NASDAQ universe and narrow results according to the characteristics you want to study.
Breakout Scanner Active breakouts Research stocks that have already moved beyond identified resistance and compare the quality of the structure behind the move.
Pre-Breakout Scanner Pressure beneath resistance Study developing structures with repeated resistance tests and improving lows before a confirmed breakout occurs.
Launch Pad Earlier technical bases Explore longer-developing ranges and bases that may still be several stages away from an active breakout.
Step 3 · Open the chart

Move from ranking to evidence.

Whether a stock came from the top six or a manual scanner, the next step is the chart.

Use EdgeBreak's integrated TradingView workspace to compare trend, support, resistance, higher lows, volume and the relationship between current price and the original base.

The drag-and-drop chart workflow makes it faster to move through several candidates during the same research session.

Use the same chart checklist every time

  • What is the weekly trend?
  • Where are the important support and resistance zones?
  • Are higher lows developing?
  • Is the structure orderly, tightening or becoming erratic?
  • How is volume behaving?
  • Is price still close to the underlying base or already extended?
  • Does unusual market activity support or conflict with the technical picture?
Step 4 · Deep research

Open AI Company Research.

Once the chart deserves deeper investigation, use the AI Company Research button.

This is the deeper research layer for understanding the business behind the ticker. It can organise company overview, financial highlights, recent news, relevant filings, institutional activity context and key risk areas into a faster research starting point.

Use it to identify what deserves verification and further reading, not as a substitute for independent research.

Step 5 · AI chart review

Use AI Chart Analysis as a second set of eyes.

AI Chart Analysis describes the visible technical structure in the chart.

It can help organise observations around trend, support, resistance, volume and current structure, but it is intentionally descriptive rather than a buy, sell or hold system.

Compare the AI description with your own chart interpretation and investigate any differences.

Guided AI vs AI Chat

EdgeBreak now gives members two different conversational research experiences.

They are useful for different reasons, so do not treat them as the same tool.

Guided AI Structured questions Use Guided AI when you want a controlled path through common stock, technical-analysis, NASDAQ, sector, account or learning questions without having to decide how to phrase the prompt.
AI Chat Open-ended conversation Use conversational AI when you want to ask follow-up questions, explore a stock in context, clarify terminology or continue a research discussion in your own words.

Use AI to ask better questions

The most useful AI workflow is not asking, “Will this stock go up?”

Use AI to investigate questions such as:

  • What does this company actually do?
  • What changed in the latest reported financial period?
  • Are revenue and earnings moving in the same direction?
  • What recent company developments could explain increased market attention?
  • Are there dilution, debt or balance-sheet risks worth investigating?
  • What technical structure is visible on the chart?
  • Which evidence should I verify independently?
Step 6 · Build the watchlist

Save only what deserves another look.

After the chart and company research, save the strongest candidates to My Workspace.

My Workspace is where discovery becomes ongoing research. You can reopen saved stocks, compare charts and continue investigating how the original structure changes over time.

A focused list is more useful than saving every interesting ticker.

Step 7 — Revisit the evidence

Research should not end when a stock is saved.

When you revisit the candidate, compare the current evidence with the reason it originally stood out.

  • Has the technical structure improved or weakened?
  • Is resistance still relevant?
  • Are higher lows still developing?
  • Has volume behaviour changed?
  • Has unusual market activity changed?
  • Has new company information changed the research picture?
  • Would you still save this stock today?

Where each AI layer fits

EdgeBreak does not use one AI button for everything.

The Automated Daily Scan uses AI-assisted research to add context to the final shortlist. AI Company Research is the deeper company-research layer. AI Chart Analysis focuses on the visible chart. Guided AI provides controlled questions, while AI Chat supports more open-ended follow-up conversation.

Used together, they reduce repetitive research work while still leaving the final interpretation with the member.

Lesson summary

The modern EdgeBreak workflow begins with automated discovery and research, then moves into manual scanner exploration, TradingView chart analysis, AI Company Research, AI Chart Analysis, Guided AI, conversational AI Chat and My Workspace. Each layer reduces research friction while keeping the final interpretation in the member's hands.

Key takeaways

  • The Automated Daily Scan is now the fastest starting point.
  • The top six combine technical, market-activity and company research evidence.
  • Manual scanners remain important for broader discovery.
  • Always inspect the chart behind the ranking.
  • Use the right AI tool for the right research question.
  • My Workspace turns discovery into ongoing research.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Treating the top six as recommendations.
  • Skipping chart review because automated research is available.
  • Asking AI for predictions instead of evidence.
  • Using AI research without verifying important facts.
  • Saving too many stocks without a clear research reason.
