The EdgeBreak workflow

Every major feature inside EdgeBreak is designed to support a structured research process rather than encourage impulsive decisions. Instead of manually searching thousands of charts, the platform helps narrow the NASDAQ and organise the information that deserves a closer look.

You do not need to use every tool on every stock. The purpose of the platform is to move from broad discovery to focused investigation, then save the strongest research candidates so you can revisit them as the structure develops.

A simple way to think about EdgeBreak is: discover → investigate → organise → revisit.

Automated Daily Scan

The Automated Daily Scan is the fastest place to begin. After the U.S. market closes, EdgeBreak scans more than 3,200 NASDAQ-listed stocks, applies its technical and research filters, assesses unusual market activity and surfaces a focused group of six stocks for further investigation.

The six stocks are not recommendations. They are a daily research starting point: a small group that has already passed through the EdgeBreak filtering process so you can spend more time investigating and less time searching.

NASDAQ Scanner

The NASDAQ Scanner is the flagship manual research workspace. It allows you to explore scanner results yourself, load stocks into the integrated chart, continue company research and move interesting candidates into your own workspace.

Rather than searching charts one by one, the scanner helps narrow the market to stocks displaying technical characteristics that may deserve further investigation.

Breakout & Pre-Breakout scanners

The Breakout Scanner focuses on stocks that have moved through a defined resistance area according to the EdgeBreak model. The Pre-Breakout Scanner looks earlier, searching for developing structures such as repeated resistance interaction, higher lows and tightening price action.

These tools are useful for studying different stages of a setup. One helps you investigate structures that have already broken out, while the other helps you study stocks that may still be developing beneath resistance.

Launch Pad

Launch Pad searches for stocks building organised bases and trading ranges. It looks for support and resistance behaviour that has remained relatively contained over time, helping surface stocks that may be compressing inside a defined structure.

Launch Pad is another discovery tool, not a prediction system. A stock appearing there still needs to be investigated in context.

Market activity context

After a technical setup is identified, EdgeBreak can add another layer of evidence by examining unusual off-exchange activity and behaviour across multiple FINRA reporting venues.

This information can help show where activity is unusual compared with a stock's own recent history. It does not identify whether the activity was buying or selling and it does not prove institutional accumulation. It is market context that can be considered alongside the chart and other research.

AI Company Research

AI Company Research helps you move beyond the chart by organising information about the company itself. Depending on the available information, research can include financial highlights, company background, recent news, institutional context and SEC filings.

AI is used to help organise research, not to replace verification. Important information should still be checked against reliable company disclosures and official sources.

AI Chart Analysis

AI Chart Analysis allows you to upload or paste a chart screenshot and receive a descriptive technical breakdown. It can help explain visible support and resistance, consolidation, trend structure and volume behaviour in straightforward language.

The analysis is educational and descriptive. It does not provide buy, sell or hold instructions and should be considered alongside your own chart work.

Guided & Conversational AI

Guided AI provides structured questions for common research topics and platform features. Conversational AI gives you more flexibility to ask your own questions and continue exploring a stock or research concept naturally.

These tools are designed to make the research process easier to understand, especially when you are still learning the language of technical analysis and market research.

My Workspace

My Workspace is your personal research area. Stocks that interest you can be saved from the scanners, organised into a watchlist and reopened later for further investigation.

The idea is simple: save → organise → revisit → research. Interesting stocks do not disappear just because the daily scan changes. You can continue watching how the chart and company story develop over time.

TradingView charts

Integrated TradingView charts allow you to study price action without leaving the EdgeBreak research workflow. You can zoom through historical data, change timeframes and use charting tools to investigate support, resistance, trends and volume.

This is where many of the concepts taught throughout the Academy become practical. As you progress through later modules, you will use charts to recognise the structures and behaviours discussed in each lesson.

Building a daily research routine

You do not need to spend all day searching the market. A short, repeatable process can help keep your research focused and consistent.

  • Start with the Automated Daily Scan and review why the six stocks were surfaced.
  • Use the manual scanners when you want to explore additional NASDAQ setups yourself.
  • Open charts and investigate the technical structure.
  • Review market activity and company research when deeper context is useful.
  • Save interesting stocks to My Workspace.
  • Revisit your watchlist and observe how the setups change over time.

Over time, this routine can help reduce unnecessary market noise and make the research process easier to repeat.

Lesson summary

EdgeBreak brings stock discovery, technical structure, unusual market activity, AI-assisted research, TradingView charting and personal organisation into one connected workflow. The purpose is to help you move from a very large NASDAQ universe to a smaller group of stocks that you can investigate independently and revisit over time.

Key takeaways

  • The Automated Daily Scan provides a focused daily starting point.
  • Manual scanners let you explore Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad structures yourself.
  • Market activity and AI research add context after a stock has been discovered.
  • My Workspace helps you save, organise and revisit research candidates.
  • EdgeBreak tools support — rather than replace — independent research.
