Your personal research hub

My Workspace is designed to become the centre of your EdgeBreak research process.

Instead of repeatedly searching for the same companies, you can save the strongest research candidates and return to them as new price, volume and company information appears.

Think of it as your own organised research library rather than a brokerage portfolio.

Discovery finds the stock. My Workspace gives you somewhere to keep the research alive.

Managing your watchlist

Your watchlist should contain stocks that still have a clear reason for being researched.

Rather than saving every interesting ticker, focus on a manageable group of companies with technical structure, company information or market activity that you genuinely want to follow.

Review the list regularly and remove candidates when the evidence changes or the original research reason no longer applies.

Save Keep the strongest candidates Add stocks when there is a clear research reason for wanting to revisit them.
Review Watch the structure evolve Return to saved stocks as new market and company information becomes available.
Remove Keep the list relevant Delete candidates when the original structure weakens or the research case is no longer useful.

Opening charts from your watchlist

Selecting a stock from My Workspace loads its integrated TradingView chart so you can move quickly between saved candidates.

Use the chart to review:

  • Broader market structure.
  • Support and resistance.
  • Higher lows and trend development.
  • Volume behaviour.
  • Weekly and daily timeframes.
  • Whether the original setup is strengthening, weakening or changing.

Use TradingView tools for your own analysis

The charting workspace includes tools for drawing trendlines, marking support and resistance and switching between different timeframes.

These tools are valuable because they keep the interpretation in your hands. The platform can narrow the market and organise information, but your own chart review remains an important part of the process.

Review market structure the same way each time

Consistency becomes much easier when every saved stock is reviewed using the same questions.

  • Is the broader trend still healthy?
  • Are higher lows continuing?
  • Has resistance changed?
  • Has the range tightened or expanded?
  • How is volume behaving?
  • Has unusual market activity changed?
  • Does the stock still deserve a place on the watchlist?

Using a repeatable checklist helps reduce the temptation to interpret every stock differently after the fact.

Connect My Workspace with AI research

A saved stock can also become the starting point for deeper company or chart investigation.

Revisit the stock from more than one angle.

After reviewing the chart, use EdgeBreak's AI-assisted research tools when additional context is useful.

AI Company Research Review company overview, financial highlights, recent news, institutional activity context and relevant filings.
AI Chart Analysis Review a descriptive analysis of visible technical structure, trend, resistance, support and volume behaviour.

Keep your watchlist evolving

Markets change every day, so a useful watchlist should change with them.

Some stocks will continue developing while others lose their structure, move too far from the original base or simply become less useful for your research.

A weekly clean-up keeps My Workspace focused and prevents it from turning into a permanent list of old tickers.

A current EdgeBreak daily routine

The most useful way to think about My Workspace is as the final stage of a larger research loop.

One connected research routine

Scan → chart → research → save.

01 Scan Review the Automated Daily Scan or use Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad scanners.
02 Chart Move candidates into TradingView and compare their technical structure.
03 Research Use AI Company Research or AI Chart Analysis when deeper context is useful.
04 Save Add the strongest research candidates to My Workspace and revisit them over time.

My Workspace is not a brokerage portfolio

My Workspace is designed for research organisation.

It does not place orders, execute trades, manage positions or recommend portfolio allocations.

Its purpose is to help you keep independent research organised and make it easier to compare how different NASDAQ candidates develop over time.

Practical exercise

Clean up and rebuild your workspace.

Spend 30 minutes reviewing the stocks currently saved in My Workspace.

  • Open every saved stock.
  • Review the weekly and daily chart structure.
  • Remove any stock that no longer has a clear research reason.
  • Open the latest Automated Daily Scan or scanners.
  • Add two fresh candidates that deserve ongoing review.
  • Use AI Company Research or AI Chart Analysis on one saved stock.
  • Finish with a focused watchlist you can realistically monitor.

Lesson summary

My Workspace brings personal watchlists, TradingView charting and ongoing NASDAQ research into one organised environment. It allows members to save promising candidates, revisit changing market structure and connect scanner discovery with deeper chart and company research.

Key takeaways

  • Use My Workspace as your personal research hub.
  • Keep the watchlist focused and current.
  • Review saved stocks consistently using TradingView.
  • Use AI research for additional context when needed.
  • Remove stocks when the original research reason no longer applies.
  • My Workspace organises research; it does not manage trades.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Saving too many stocks.
  • Never removing outdated candidates.
  • Relying on memory instead of revisiting the chart.
  • Ignoring changes in the original market structure.
  • Treating a watchlist as a recommendation list.
