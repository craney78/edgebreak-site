Common questions

Straight answers.

What is EdgeBreak AI?

EdgeBreak AI combines automated NASDAQ stock scanning, AI company research, AI stock chart analysis and conversational research tools inside one connected platform.

Can EdgeBreak automatically scan the NASDAQ?

Yes. After each U.S. market close, EdgeBreak scans thousands of NASDAQ stocks, filters the results and presents a focused daily shortlist for further research.

Do I have to manually scan stocks myself?

No. The automated scan can complete the initial discovery and filtering process. Manual NASDAQ scanners are also available.

What can AI Company Research investigate?

Company Research can investigate company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional ownership and activity, and SEC filings.

Can EdgeBreak AI analyse my own stock charts?

Yes. Upload or paste a chart for descriptive analysis of visible market structure, support, resistance, consolidation, volume behaviour and breakout structure.

What is the difference between Guided AI and full conversation?

Guided AI provides structured questions for fast explanations. Full conversation lets you ask your own questions naturally and continue with follow-ups.

Can AI-generated research contain errors?

Yes. AI-generated information can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Important information should be independently verified using reliable and official sources.

Does EdgeBreak AI tell me what to buy?

No. EdgeBreak AI provides stock discovery, factual research, educational information and descriptive analysis. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations, price targets or investment advice.
