What to look for

Start with structure.

EdgeBreak narrows the field. Your next job is deciding whether the structure still makes sense when you inspect it.

Price Structure

Look for organised price action, constructive higher lows, defined resistance and controlled consolidation.

Volume & Participation

Compare volume behaviour with the price structure and look for evidence of participation strengthening as the setup develops.

Market Evidence

Unusual market activity can add context, but should be treated as evidence for further research rather than confirmation of intent.

Typical breakout structure

The setup before the move matters.

A breakout is easier to understand when you first recognise the base, resistance and higher-low structure that developed before it.

Before Breakout

Resistance remains defined while buyers continue stepping in at progressively higher prices beneath the level.

After Breakout

Price moves beyond resistance and the developing structure can then be reviewed against participation and follow-through.

Common questions

Straight answers.

What is the EdgeBreak Trading Guide?

It explains a repeatable research workflow for discovering, investigating and monitoring NASDAQ-listed stocks using EdgeBreak.

Where should a beginner start?

Start with the automated daily scan and EdgeBreak Academy. The scan gives you a small group of stocks to investigate, while the Academy explains the technical concepts behind the platform.

Does EdgeBreak tell me what to buy?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, research tools, educational content and descriptive analysis. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations.

What should I do after finding a stock?

Review the technical structure, investigate the company, verify important information and save stronger candidates to your watchlist or workspace for continued monitoring.

Are historical examples trading results?

No. Historical examples show structures previously identified or tracked using the EdgeBreak framework. They are educational examples and do not guarantee future performance.
