What is EdgeBreak Trading Insights?

Trading Insights is EdgeBreak's free educational library covering NASDAQ stock research, breakout structures, market structure, volume, scanners and AI-assisted research.

Is Trading Insights free?

Yes. The articles and guides in Trading Insights are free educational resources.

What should a beginner read first?

Start with the Breakout Guide for technical concepts, then use the EdgeBreak Research Guide to understand how those concepts fit into a wider research workflow.

Does Trading Insights provide financial advice?

No. Trading Insights provides educational content and research concepts only. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations.
