Common questions

Straight answers.

What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ stock discovery and research platform combining automated scanning, specialised stock scanners, AI Company Research, AI Chart Analysis, Guided AI, conversational AI and research tools.

Do I need to run the scanners myself?

No. EdgeBreak completes an automated NASDAQ scan after the U.S. market closes and presents a focused shortlist. Manual scanners are also available when you want to explore the market yourself.

Which scanners are included?

EdgeBreak includes Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad scanners, covering active breakouts, developing structures approaching resistance and organised technical bases.

What can AI Company Research investigate?

Company Research can help investigate company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional activity and SEC filings.

Can EdgeBreak analyse my own stock charts?

Yes. Upload, paste or drag and drop a chart for descriptive analysis of visible support, resistance, market structure, higher lows, consolidation and volume behaviour.

What is the difference between Standard and AI Unlimited?

Standard includes the complete EdgeBreak platform and 15 full conversational AI questions each month. AI Unlimited includes the same platform with unlimited conversational AI access.

Can beginners use EdgeBreak?

Yes. Guided AI explains common research questions in clear language, while EdgeBreak Academy teaches the technical concepts behind the platform.

Does EdgeBreak provide financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, market information, research tools and educational content. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations, price targets or investment advice.
