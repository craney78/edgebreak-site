NASDAQ Breakout Scanner

The EdgeBreak NASDAQ Scanner helps investors identify stocks that have already broken above resistance and are showing signs of momentum, volume expansion and improving market structure. These breakout opportunities can be filtered using price, liquidity, volume and performance characteristics to quickly find stocks displaying active strength.

Resistance, Higher Lows & Smart Money

EdgeBreak allows investors to search for stocks repeatedly testing resistance while forming higher lows underneath key price levels. Investors can also apply the Smart Money Filter, which identifies accumulation characteristics such as volume clustering, absorption, strong closes and tight price structure. Combining these filters can help uncover stocks displaying both accumulation activity and developing breakout structures.

Research Thousands Of NASDAQ Stocks

Search the entire NASDAQ universe using filters for resistance touches, higher lows, Smart Money signals, volume expansion, average volume, new highs, distance to highs and price groups. Scanner results are updated daily and designed to help investors build structured watchlists, identify accumulation characteristics and research potential breakout opportunities across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks.
