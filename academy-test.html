Common questions

Straight answers.

Is EdgeBreak Academy free?

Yes. EdgeBreak Academy is free and provides structured lessons covering NASDAQ stock research, technical analysis and the EdgeBreak research methodology.

Do I need trading experience?

No. The Academy begins with foundational concepts before progressing to more advanced market research topics.

How many lessons are in EdgeBreak Academy?

The current Academy structure contains 30 lessons across six modules, with most lessons designed to take around 15 to 25 minutes.

What will I learn?

The Academy covers candlesticks, support and resistance, volume, trends, accumulation, breakout research, risk concepts, real market examples and the EdgeBreak research platform.

Can I complete the Academy at my own pace?

Yes. There are no deadlines. Work through the modules in order or return to individual lessons whenever you want a refresher.

Does EdgeBreak Academy provide financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak Academy provides educational material only. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, stock ratings, price targets or buy and sell signals.
