AI stock chart analysis FAQ

Straight answers.

What is AI stock chart analysis?

AI stock chart analysis uses artificial intelligence to describe visible technical features in a stock chart, such as market structure, support, resistance, consolidation, volume behaviour and breakout structure.

Can I upload my own stock chart to EdgeBreak?

Yes. You can upload, paste or drag and drop a chart image into EdgeBreak for descriptive AI-assisted chart analysis.

What does EdgeBreak analyse on a stock chart?

EdgeBreak can describe visible market structure, support, resistance, consolidation, higher lows, volume behaviour and breakout structure where these features are visible in the supplied chart.

Can EdgeBreak analyse charts that were not found by its scanner?

Yes. Chart Analysis is not limited to stocks surfaced by EdgeBreak. You can supply your own stock chart image for analysis.

Can I use EdgeBreak AI Stock Chart Analysis in the United States, Canada or Australia?

EdgeBreak is a web-based platform. Investors researching the U.S. market from the United States, Canada, Australia and other supported regions can use the same AI-assisted stock chart analysis workflow.

Is AI stock chart analysis the same as a trading signal?

No. EdgeBreak provides descriptive analysis of the visible chart. It does not provide buy, sell or hold signals, price targets or investment advice.

Can AI chart analysis be wrong?

Yes. AI-generated analysis can contain errors or interpret chart features incorrectly. Always review the original chart and verify important information independently.
