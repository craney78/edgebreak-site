NASDAQ Stock Scanner: How to Scan Thousands of NASDAQ Stocks

August 2026 • 8 min read

A NASDAQ stock scanner automatically searches thousands of NASDAQ-listed stocks for predefined market characteristics. Instead of manually reviewing every chart, investors can use a scanner to narrow the market to a smaller list of stocks displaying technical structures such as breakouts, pre-breakouts, resistance tests, higher lows and consolidation.

What Is a NASDAQ Stock Scanner?

A NASDAQ stock scanner is software that searches NASDAQ-listed securities using predefined criteria. The scanner automatically processes market data and identifies stocks matching the selected rules.

This allows investors to begin with a smaller group of potentially relevant stocks instead of manually searching the entire market.

Why Use a Stock Scanner?

Thousands of companies trade on the NASDAQ. Even if an investor spent only one minute reviewing each chart, manually checking the entire exchange would take many hours.

Automated scanning allows the computer to perform the repetitive filtering process while the investor concentrates on deeper research.

What Can a NASDAQ Scanner Search For?

Different scanners use different rules. Technical stock scanners may search for price and volume characteristics rather than company fundamentals.

  • Breakouts above resistance.
  • Stocks approaching resistance.
  • Higher lows.
  • Repeated resistance tests.
  • Support areas.
  • Consolidation.
  • Base-forming structures.
  • Volume behaviour.

Stock Scanner vs Stock Screener

The terms stock scanner and stock screener are often used interchangeably, but they can describe slightly different approaches.

A traditional screener often filters companies using values such as market capitalisation, price, earnings or volume. A technical scanner may focus more heavily on changing price structure and chart behaviour.

Finding Breakout Stocks

One common use of a NASDAQ stock scanner is breakout discovery. Instead of manually searching for stocks trading near resistance, automated rules can identify repeated resistance interactions, higher lows and stocks already moving above established resistance areas.

Finding Stocks Before a Breakout

Scanners can also identify stocks before a confirmed breakout occurs. Pre-breakout research focuses on developing structure beneath resistance rather than waiting until price has already moved higher.

This provides investors with time to investigate the company and chart before deciding whether the stock deserves ongoing attention.

Combining Scanning With AI Research

Finding a technically interesting stock is only the beginning of the research process. Investors may also want to understand the company, recent financial performance, market developments and the technical chart.

Combining stock discovery with AI-powered research creates a more complete workflow: scan the market, identify stocks, investigate the company and analyse the chart.
