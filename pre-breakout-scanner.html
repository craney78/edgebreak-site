Pre-breakout scanner FAQ

Straight answers.

What is a pre-breakout stock scanner?

A pre-breakout stock scanner looks for stocks developing technical structure near resistance before a confirmed breakout occurs. EdgeBreak focuses on NASDAQ stocks with resistance, higher lows and tightening price action that may deserve further research.

What does the EdgeBreak Pre-Breakout Scanner look for?

EdgeBreak looks for developing structures near resistance, including repeated resistance interaction, higher lows, consolidation and tightening price behaviour.

Is Pre-Breakout different from the Breakout Scanner?

Yes. Pre-Breakout focuses on stocks still developing below or near resistance. Breakout Scanner focuses on stocks that have already moved through established resistance.

Can I use the Pre-Breakout Scanner in the United States, Canada or Australia?

Yes. EdgeBreak is web-based and focused on NASDAQ-listed stocks. Investors researching the U.S. market from the United States, Canada, Australia and other supported regions can use the same pre-breakout scanner.

Does the scanner find stocks near resistance?

That is one of its core purposes. The scanner is designed to surface NASDAQ stocks with developing technical structure around established resistance so they can be investigated before confirmation.

Does a pre-breakout scanner predict which stocks will break out?

No. It identifies stocks matching defined technical conditions before a breakout. The setup may fail, remain in consolidation or never break resistance.
