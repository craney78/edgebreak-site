Common questions

Straight answers.

What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ stock discovery and research platform combining automated scanning, AI company research, AI chart analysis and specialised NASDAQ stock scanners.

What market does EdgeBreak cover?

EdgeBreak focuses on more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks, giving the platform a specialised and consistent research universe.

Does EdgeBreak automatically scan stocks for me?

Yes. After each U.S. market close, EdgeBreak scans thousands of NASDAQ stocks, filters the results and surfaces a focused group for further investigation.

Can I still run the scanners myself?

Yes. Automated discovery and manual NASDAQ stock scanning are both available, allowing you to use EdgeBreak as a starting point or run your own searches.

What research tools are included?

EdgeBreak includes automated NASDAQ scanning, Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad scanners, AI Company Research, AI Chart Analysis, Smart Money research, TradingView charts, watchlists, My Workspace and EdgeBreak Academy.

Who is EdgeBreak designed for?

EdgeBreak is designed for independent investors who want a more structured and efficient way to discover and investigate NASDAQ stocks.

Does EdgeBreak provide financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, factual market research, AI-powered research tools and educational information. It does not provide investment recommendations, stock ratings, price targets or buy and sell signals.
