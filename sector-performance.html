Why sector performance matters

A stock does not trade in isolation.

Sector performance can add useful context when researching individual stocks. A technically strong company inside an improving sector may be operating in a different environment from a similar setup inside a weakening group.

Sector strength should be considered alongside trend, price structure, volume and broader market conditions rather than used as a standalone signal.

How EdgeBreak uses it

Context, not confirmation.

EdgeBreak uses broader market and sector information as another research layer. It can help organise and prioritise investigation, but it does not prove institutional buying or predict what an individual stock will do next.
