Why EdgeBreak Exists

Researching the NASDAQ can mean sorting through thousands of stocks, charts, company announcements, financial results and market developments just to find what deserves a closer look.

EdgeBreak was created to simplify that process.

Instead of starting with thousands of stocks, EdgeBreak automatically scans more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed companies for Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Base Forming structures, filters the results and uses AI research to investigate the companies it finds.

The idea is simple: let technology do the first stage of the research, then give investors the tools to investigate further.

EdgeBreak combines automated NASDAQ scanning with AI company research, AI chart analysis and manual stock scanners in one research platform. Users can review stocks surfaced by EdgeBreak, investigate individual companies with AI, analyse charts or run their own scans.

By focusing exclusively on NASDAQ-listed stocks, EdgeBreak provides a specialised research environment rather than attempting to cover every market, exchange and asset class.

EdgeBreak is not designed to predict markets or tell investors what to buy or sell. It is designed to make stock discovery and research faster, more structured and easier to investigate independently.

The goal is simple:

Scan the NASDAQ. Surface what deserves attention. Make deeper research easier.

How EdgeBreak Works

EdgeBreak begins by scanning thousands of NASDAQ-listed stocks using predefined technical criteria including market structure, support and resistance, higher lows, volume behaviour and breakout characteristics.

The resulting stocks are filtered before EdgeBreak AI researches the remaining companies for current market attention, recent news and company-specific developments. A focused group is then surfaced for further investigation.

From there, you decide how deep to go. Open AI Company Research, analyse a chart with EdgeBreak AI, review charts and scanner data, or use the manual scanners to conduct your own searches.

AI Company Research

EdgeBreak AI Company Research lets you investigate an individual company without leaving the platform. Research sections cover company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional ownership and activity, and SEC filings.

Research is available on demand, allowing you to choose the areas you want to investigate rather than working through unnecessary information.

AI Chart Analysis

Upload, paste or drag and drop a stock chart into EdgeBreak AI for descriptive technical analysis of the structure visible on the chart.

AI Chart Analysis can examine market structure, support, resistance, higher lows, consolidation, volume behaviour and breakout structure. You can analyse stocks discovered through EdgeBreak or use your own charts.

Manual NASDAQ Scanners

Want to do the scanning yourself? EdgeBreak's manual scanners remain available for investors who want direct control over stock discovery.
