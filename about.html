Why EdgeBreak Exists

Financial markets generate an enormous amount of information every day. Thousands of stocks, company announcements, financial results, news, market commentary and technical data can make it difficult for independent investors to identify what deserves further research.

EdgeBreak was created to simplify that process.

Rather than requiring investors to manually review hundreds of charts each day, EdgeBreak applies proprietary stock scanning and pattern recognition technology across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks to identify developing market structures, accumulation characteristics, technical bases and potential breakout opportunities.

The platform was built on a simple belief: better decisions start with better research.

EdgeBreak now brings stock discovery and research together in one platform. Stocks identified through the EdgeBreak scanners can be investigated further using EdgeBreak AI Research, providing focused research across company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional activity and SEC filings.

By focusing exclusively on NASDAQ-listed stocks, EdgeBreak provides a targeted and consistent research experience rather than attempting to cover every market, exchange and asset class.

Transparency remains a core part of the platform. Opportunities identified through the EdgeBreak framework are tracked over time, allowing users to review both successful and unsuccessful outcomes through a consistent historical record.

EdgeBreak is not designed to predict markets or tell investors which stocks to buy or sell. Its purpose is to help investors discover opportunities, organise information, conduct independent research and better understand the companies and market structures they are investigating.

The goal is simple:

To make structured NASDAQ stock discovery and research more accessible through technology, AI, data, transparency and consistency.

How the Platform Works

EdgeBreak applies a structured end-of-day scanning process across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks. Market data is analysed using predefined price action, volume, support and resistance, market structure and pattern recognition criteria to identify stocks for further research.

Stocks discovered through the EdgeBreak scanners can then be investigated further using charts, watchlists and EdgeBreak AI Research. The AI Research panel provides focused research covering company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional activity and SEC filings.

This approach brings stock discovery and company research together in one platform, helping investors maintain a consistent research process while reducing the time required to manually search, organise and investigate NASDAQ opportunities.

Why We Focus On NASDAQ

EdgeBreak focuses exclusively on NASDAQ-listed stocks. By concentrating on a single market of more than 3,000 companies, the platform can apply a consistent discovery and research framework across technology, healthcare, biotechnology, growth and innovation-focused companies.

This specialised approach allows EdgeBreak to focus deeply on one market, providing a more consistent stock discovery and research experience rather than attempting to cover every exchange, market and asset class.
