Why EdgeBreak Exists

Financial markets generate an enormous amount of information every day. Thousands of stocks, company announcements, financial results, news, market commentary and technical data can make it difficult for independent investors to identify what deserves further research.

EdgeBreak was created to simplify that process.

Rather than requiring investors to manually review hundreds of charts each day, EdgeBreak applies stock scanning and advanced pattern recognition across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks to identify market structures, technical bases, volume characteristics and potential breakout structures for further research.

The platform was built on a simple belief: better decisions start with better research.

EdgeBreak combines a NASDAQ Stock Scanner with AI-powered stock research in one structured platform. Stocks discovered through the EdgeBreak scanners can be investigated further using EdgeBreak AI Stock Research, covering company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional ownership and activity, and SEC filings.

By focusing on NASDAQ-listed stocks, EdgeBreak provides a targeted and consistent stock discovery and research experience rather than attempting to cover every market, exchange and asset class.

Transparency remains an important part of the EdgeBreak approach. Market structures and stocks identified through the EdgeBreak research framework can be reviewed over time, helping investors examine both successful and unsuccessful outcomes as part of their own research.

EdgeBreak is not designed to predict markets or tell investors which stocks to buy or sell. Its purpose is to help investors discover stocks, organise information, investigate companies and conduct their own independent market research.

The goal is simple:

To make structured NASDAQ stock discovery and AI-powered stock research more accessible through technology, data, transparency and consistency.

How the Platform Works

EdgeBreak applies a structured stock scanning process across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks. Market data is analysed using predefined price action, volume, support and resistance, higher low, market structure and pattern recognition criteria to identify stocks for further research.

Stocks discovered through the EdgeBreak NASDAQ Stock Scanner and specialised scanners can then be investigated further using TradingView charts, personal watchlists and EdgeBreak AI Stock Research. The AI Research panel provides focused research covering company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news, institutional ownership and activity, and SEC filings.

This creates a connected research workflow: discover stocks through specialised NASDAQ scanners, investigate the company with AI-powered stock research, then organise and continue your independent analysis using charts and watchlists.

Why We Focus On NASDAQ

EdgeBreak focuses on NASDAQ-listed stocks. By concentrating on a single market of more than 3,000 companies, the platform can apply a consistent stock scanning and research framework across a large universe of technology, healthcare, biotechnology, growth and other listed companies.

This specialised approach allows EdgeBreak to focus deeply on one market, providing a consistent NASDAQ stock discovery and research experience rather than attempting to cover every exchange, market and asset class.
