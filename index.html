Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about EdgeBreak AI, automated NASDAQ scanning, AI stock research, AI chart analysis and EdgeBreak's manual research tools.

What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ market research platform combining automated stock scanning, AI stock research, AI chart analysis and manual stock scanners. EdgeBreak is designed to help investors reduce thousands of NASDAQ stocks to a more focused research process.

What is EdgeBreak Automated AI Stock Research?

After each U.S. market close, EdgeBreak automatically scans thousands of NASDAQ stocks for Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Base Forming setups. The stocks identified by the scanners are filtered before AI market research investigates the remaining companies for noteworthy market attention, news and recent company developments.

Do I have to manually scan the NASDAQ myself?

No. EdgeBreak can complete the initial scanning, filtering and AI research process automatically. Members who prefer to conduct their own searches can also use the EdgeBreak Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Base Forming scanners manually.

What are the EdgeBreak NASDAQ Scanners?

The EdgeBreak NASDAQ Scanners search thousands of NASDAQ-listed stocks for technical structures including breakouts above resistance, stocks approaching resistance while forming higher lows and longer bases developing beneath resistance.

What is Smart Money Analysis?

EdgeBreak Smart Money analysis provides additional historical scanner information that can be used when researching NASDAQ stocks and filtering scanner results.

What is EdgeBreak AI Company Research?

EdgeBreak AI Company Research allows members to investigate individual stocks directly inside the platform. Research can cover company information, financial highlights, market attention, recent news and developments, institutional activity and SEC filings.

What is EdgeBreak AI Chart Analysis?

EdgeBreak AI Chart Analysis allows you to upload or drag and drop a stock chart for descriptive analysis of the technical characteristics visible in the chart, including market structure, support, resistance, consolidation and volume behaviour.

Does EdgeBreak AI recommend which stocks to buy?

No. EdgeBreak AI provides stock discovery, factual research and descriptive analysis. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations, investment ratings, price targets or promises about future stock performance.

Can EdgeBreak AI make mistakes?

Yes. AI-generated information can contain errors, incomplete information or outdated information. Important information should be independently verified using reliable sources, company disclosures and official regulatory filings.

Can beginners use EdgeBreak?

Yes. EdgeBreak is designed to make NASDAQ stock discovery and research easier to understand, while EdgeBreak Academy provides structured lessons covering stock scanning, technical analysis, market structure, breakouts, risk management and independent research.

Does EdgeBreak provide investing education?

Yes. Visit the EdgeBreak Academy, Trading Guide, Breakout Guide, Strategy and Trading Insights to learn about stock scanning, technical analysis, market structure and independent stock research.

Is EdgeBreak financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak provides automated stock discovery, research tools, market information and educational content only. EdgeBreak does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, buy or sell signals, price targets or predictions. All information is provided for research and educational purposes.
