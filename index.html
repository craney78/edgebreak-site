Common questions

Straight answers.

What does EdgeBreak do?

EdgeBreak scans the NASDAQ for technical structures, applies rules-based filters, assesses off-exchange and multi-venue activity, and presents six stocks for independent research after each market close.

Does EdgeBreak confirm institutional buying?

No. FINRA data is delayed and does not identify buy or sell direction. EdgeBreak uses careful language to describe possible institutional-scale behaviour inferred from unusual activity.

Does EdgeBreak tell me what to buy?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, market information, educational tools and descriptive analysis. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations.

Can I run the scanners myself?

Yes. The automated scan provides a focused daily starting point, while the platform also gives you access to manual scanners and deeper research tools.

Can beginners use EdgeBreak?

Yes. Reports use clear language, Guided AI helps explain results, and EdgeBreak Academy teaches the concepts behind the platform.
