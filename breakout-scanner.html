Breakout scanner FAQ

Straight answers.

What is a breakout stock scanner?

A breakout stock scanner searches for stocks that have moved through an established resistance area. EdgeBreak uses rules-based filters to surface NASDAQ stocks with confirmed resistance breakouts for further research.

What does the EdgeBreak Breakout Scanner look for?

EdgeBreak focuses on confirmed resistance breakouts and the surrounding technical structure, including resistance behaviour, price structure and volume context.

Is this different from the Pre-Breakout Scanner?

Yes. The Breakout Scanner focuses on stocks that have already moved through resistance, while the Pre-Breakout Scanner is designed to identify developing structures before a confirmed breakout.

Can I use the Breakout Scanner in the United States, Canada or Australia?

Yes. EdgeBreak is web-based and focused on NASDAQ-listed stocks. Investors researching the U.S. market from the United States, Canada, Australia and other supported regions can use the same breakout scanner.

Does a breakout scanner predict which stocks will keep rising?

No. A scanner identifies stocks matching defined technical conditions. A confirmed breakout can still fail, reverse or move sideways.

Does EdgeBreak provide buy or sell signals?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, technical research and educational information. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations, price targets or investment advice.
