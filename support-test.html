Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ stock discovery and research platform combining automated scanning, specialised stock scanners, AI company research, AI chart analysis, workspace tools and education.

Does EdgeBreak provide financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, factual market research, AI-assisted research tools and educational information. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations or buy and sell signals.

How often are the scanners updated?

EdgeBreak processes its automated scanning and end-of-day research after each U.S. trading session using the latest available market data.

What tools are included?

EdgeBreak includes automated NASDAQ scanning, Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad scanners, AI Company Research, AI Chart Analysis, Smart Money research, My Workspace, watchlists, TradingView charts and EdgeBreak Academy.

Where should I start if I am new?

Start with EdgeBreak Academy to understand the concepts behind the platform, then explore the NASDAQ scanners and wider research tools.

How do I contact EdgeBreak support?

Email admin@edgebreak.ai with your question and any useful details about the issue.
