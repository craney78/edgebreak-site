Before you continue

Cookies support the operation of the platform.

This Cookies Policy explains how EdgeBreak uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the platform, improve performance, remember preferences and help protect your account.

EdgeBreak is operated by Crane Platform Labs. Certain cookies are required for secure authentication and core platform functionality, while other cookies primarily support convenience, analytics and improvement.

Purpose of This Cookies Policy

This Cookies Policy explains how EdgeBreak uses cookies and similar technologies to operate our platform, enhance your browsing experience, improve security and deliver core website functionality. It also explains the choices available to you regarding the use of cookies.

Cookies help us provide secure authentication, remember your preferences, improve platform performance and ensure important features continue to operate as intended.

By using EdgeBreak, you acknowledge that certain cookies are required for the operation and security of the platform. Where optional cookies are used, we aim to provide clear information about their purpose and your available choices.

Supporting a secure experience Cookies help EdgeBreak keep your account secure, maintain your login session, remember your preferences and provide a reliable experience while using the platform.

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies are required for EdgeBreak to operate securely and correctly. They enable core platform functionality such as user authentication, secure account access, session management and other features that allow you to use our services safely and reliably.

  • Secure Authentication – Essential cookies help verify your identity and keep you securely signed in while using your EdgeBreak account.
  • Session Management – These cookies maintain your active session as you move between pages, preventing unnecessary interruptions while using the platform.
  • Platform Functionality – Core features such as your dashboard, watchlists, scanner access, account settings and subscription services rely on essential cookies to function correctly.
  • Security Protection – Essential cookies assist in protecting your account against unauthorised access, fraudulent activity and other security threats.
  • Required for Service – Because these cookies are necessary for the operation of EdgeBreak, disabling them may prevent parts of the platform from functioning correctly or may stop you from accessing your account.

Performance & Analytics Cookies

Performance and analytics cookies help us understand how EdgeBreak is used so we can improve the speed, reliability and overall user experience of the platform. These cookies collect information about website usage rather than being intended to directly identify individual users.

  • Platform Performance – These cookies help us monitor website performance, identify technical issues and improve the stability of EdgeBreak.
  • Usage Insights – We use aggregated information to understand which pages and features are most frequently used, helping us improve the platform over time.
  • User Experience – Performance cookies help us identify areas where navigation, loading times and overall usability can be enhanced.
  • Continuous Improvement – Information collected through these cookies assists us in developing new features and improving existing tools, educational resources and platform functionality.
  • Aggregated Data – Where analytics cookies are used, information is generally collected in an aggregated or anonymised form and is not intended to directly identify individual users.
  • Your Choice – Depending on your location and browser settings, you may be able to manage or disable certain analytics cookies, although doing so may limit our ability to improve your experience.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies allow EdgeBreak to remember your settings and provide a more personalised experience.

  • Remembering Preferences – Preference cookies remember settings such as display options, user preferences and other personalised features where available.
  • Improved User Experience – These cookies help deliver a smoother and more consistent experience by retaining your chosen settings between visits.
  • Personalised Features – As EdgeBreak evolves, preference cookies may be used to remember dashboard layouts, notification settings and other user-selected options.
  • Convenience – By remembering your preferences, these cookies reduce the need to repeatedly enter the same information each time you access the platform.
  • Your Control – You can manage or remove preference cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may reset your saved preferences.
  • Optional Functionality – Unlike essential cookies, preference cookies primarily enhance your experience and are not generally required for the core operation of EdgeBreak.

Third-Party Cookies & Platform Services

EdgeBreak works with trusted third-party service providers to deliver important platform functionality, including secure authentication, subscription management, cloud infrastructure and other essential services. Some of these providers may use cookies or similar technologies to help deliver their services.

  • Authentication Services – Our secure authentication provider may use cookies to verify your identity, maintain your login session and help protect your account from unauthorised access.
  • Payment Processing – Trusted payment providers may use cookies to securely process subscriptions, prevent fraud and manage payment transactions.
  • Cloud Infrastructure – Certain cookies may support the reliable operation, security and performance of the cloud services that power the EdgeBreak platform.
  • Embedded Services – Where EdgeBreak includes third-party content or integrations, those providers may use their own cookies in accordance with their individual privacy and cookies policies.
  • Independent Providers – Third-party cookies are controlled by the relevant service provider. EdgeBreak does not control how these providers collect, use or store information through their own cookies.
  • Third-Party Policies – We encourage you to review the privacy and cookies policies of any third-party services you interact with while using EdgeBreak.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have control over how cookies are used on your device. Most web browsers allow you to view, manage, block or delete cookies through their settings. While you can choose to restrict certain cookies, doing so may affect the functionality and performance of the EdgeBreak platform.

  • Browser Settings – Most browsers allow you to control cookie settings, including accepting, blocking or deleting cookies that have already been stored on your device.
  • Essential Cookies – Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from signing in, accessing your account or using important EdgeBreak features.
  • Preference Cookies – Removing preference cookies may reset your saved settings, requiring you to reconfigure certain options each time you visit EdgeBreak.
  • Performance Cookies – Disabling analytics or performance cookies will not normally affect core functionality, but it may limit our ability to improve the platform and user experience.
  • Third-Party Cookies – Some third-party services may provide their own tools or browser settings for managing cookies associated with their services.
  • Your Choice – You can update your browser's cookie settings at any time, although certain features of EdgeBreak may not function as intended if cookies are restricted or disabled.
You're in control You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time. Restricting certain cookies may reduce functionality or prevent some EdgeBreak features from operating correctly.

Cookie Retention

Cookies may remain on your device for different periods depending on their purpose. Some cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser, while others remain for longer to remember preferences, improve convenience and support future visits to EdgeBreak.

  • Session Cookies – These temporary cookies exist only while your browser is open and are automatically removed when your browsing session ends.
  • Persistent Cookies – Some cookies remain on your device for a defined period to remember your preferences, improve convenience and support future visits to EdgeBreak.
  • Essential Cookies – Essential cookies are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide secure authentication, maintain sessions and support core platform functionality.
  • Third-Party Cookies – Cookies placed by third-party service providers are subject to their own retention periods and privacy practices.
  • Browser Controls – You can remove stored cookies at any time through your browser settings or configure your browser to automatically delete cookies when you close it.
  • Policy Updates – Cookie retention periods may change as EdgeBreak introduces new features, improves security or updates the services provided by trusted third-party partners.

Your Consent & Choices

By continuing to use EdgeBreak, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Cookies Policy and the use of cookies and similar technologies described in this policy, subject to your browser settings and applicable laws.

  • Acceptance of This Policy – By accessing or using EdgeBreak, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Cookies Policy.
  • Cookie Use – Where permitted by law, your continued use of the EdgeBreak platform may indicate acknowledgement of the cookie practices described in this policy.
  • Browser Controls – You may manage your cookie preferences at any time by changing your browser settings or deleting stored cookies from your device.
  • Platform Functionality – Restricting or disabling certain cookies may affect the availability, performance or functionality of some EdgeBreak features and services.
  • Reviewing This Policy – We encourage you to review this Cookies Policy periodically so you remain informed about how cookies are used on the EdgeBreak platform.

Changes to This Cookies Policy

As EdgeBreak continues to evolve, we may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes to our platform, the technologies we use, third-party service providers, legal requirements or industry practices.

  • Platform Development – Updates may reflect new platform features, technologies, cookies, third-party integrations or other EdgeBreak services.
  • Legal & Regulatory Changes – This Cookies Policy may be updated where necessary to comply with applicable privacy laws, cookie regulations or recognised industry standards.
  • Improved Transparency – We may revise this Cookies Policy to provide clearer information about how cookies and similar technologies are used across the EdgeBreak platform.
  • Publication of Changes – Updates will be published on this page and the Last Updated date may be revised where applicable.
Staying informed We encourage you to review this Cookies Policy periodically so you remain informed about changes to EdgeBreak's use of cookies and similar technologies.
