Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about EdgeBreak, our NASDAQ Stock Scanners, Smart Money Filter, AI Research and market research platform

What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ stock discovery and research platform that combines specialised stock scanners, pattern recognition, Smart Money analysis and AI-powered research to help independent investors discover and investigate opportunities across more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks. Learn more on the About EdgeBreak page.

Does EdgeBreak provide financial advice?

No. EdgeBreak provides stock discovery, market research, AI-powered research tools and educational resources. It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, stock ratings, price targets or buy and sell signals. Please review our Disclaimer for more information.

What is the NASDAQ Scanner?

The NASDAQ Scanner allows investors to search more than 3,000 NASDAQ-listed stocks using pattern recognition and specialised filters based on price action, resistance touches, higher lows, Smart Money characteristics, volume behaviour, market structure and breakout patterns. Explore the NASDAQ Scanner.

What is the Smart Money Filter?

The Smart Money Filter identifies stocks displaying accumulation characteristics such as volume clustering, absorption, strong closes and tight price structure. Learn more about the Smart Money Filter and how it integrates with the NASDAQ Scanner.

What is EdgeBreak AI Research?

EdgeBreak AI Research is an AI-powered stock research tool integrated directly into the EdgeBreak platform. After discovering a stock through the EdgeBreak scanners, investors can open the AI Research panel and investigate the company behind the stock through a focused research workflow.

What can EdgeBreak AI Research investigate?

EdgeBreak AI Research can investigate company information, financial highlights, current market attention and recent news, institutional ownership and activity, and SEC filings. Research sections can be selected individually, allowing investors to focus on the information they want to investigate.

Does EdgeBreak AI tell me which stocks to buy?

No. EdgeBreak AI Research is designed to provide factual information for further research. It does not provide buy, sell or hold recommendations, stock ratings, price targets or predictions about future stock performance.

Can EdgeBreak AI Research make mistakes?

Yes. AI-generated research can contain errors, incomplete information or outdated information. Important information should always be independently verified using reliable primary sources, company disclosures and official regulatory filings before making financial decisions.

What stocks does EdgeBreak analyse?

EdgeBreak focuses exclusively on NASDAQ-listed stocks, allowing a consistent stock discovery and research framework to be applied across more than 3,000 companies. Discover more through the Trading Guide.

How do I learn the EdgeBreak framework?

Investors can explore the Trading Guide, review Trading Insights and learn how the Smart Money Filter works to better understand EdgeBreak's structured approach to stock discovery and market research.

How often is the EdgeBreak Stock Scanner updated?

EdgeBreak operates using an end-of-day scanning process. Market data and scanner results are updated after each trading session, providing members with current research opportunities across the NASDAQ market.

What is included with an EdgeBreak membership?

EdgeBreak membership includes the NASDAQ Scanner, Breakout Scanner, Pre-Breakout Scanner, Launch Pad Scanner, Smart Money Filter, full EdgeBreak AI Research panel, My Workspace, personal watchlists, TradingView chart integration and access to the platform's research tools.

Can beginners use EdgeBreak?

Yes. EdgeBreak is designed to support both developing and experienced independent investors. EdgeBreak Academy provides educational lessons covering stock scanning, technical analysis, market structure, breakout research and how to use the EdgeBreak platform.

Who is EdgeBreak designed for?

EdgeBreak is designed for independent investors who want a structured approach to discovering and researching NASDAQ-listed stocks using stock scanning, pattern recognition, technical analysis, market intelligence and AI-powered company research.
