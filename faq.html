Frequently Asked Questions

The platform, explained clearly.

EdgeBreak is built to reduce research overload, not replace your judgement. These answers explain how the platform works and where its limits are.

About EdgeBreak
What is EdgeBreak?

EdgeBreak is a NASDAQ market intelligence and stock research platform. It scans more than 3,200 NASDAQ-listed stocks, applies rules-based technical filters, assesses unusual market activity and provides tools for deeper independent research. Learn more on the About EdgeBreak page.

What does EdgeBreak scan?

EdgeBreak focuses on more than 3,200 NASDAQ-listed stocks and looks for technical structures such as breakouts, developing pre-breakouts, organised bases, reversals and consolidations.

Who is EdgeBreak designed for?

EdgeBreak is designed for independent investors who want a more structured and efficient way to discover, investigate and understand NASDAQ-listed stocks.

Scanners & Daily Research
What is the Automated Daily Scan?

After the U.S. market closes, EdgeBreak scans the NASDAQ, removes weak or unsuitable structures, adds market activity context and surfaces six stocks as a focused starting point for further research.

Why does EdgeBreak surface six stocks?

The daily process is designed to reduce a very large NASDAQ universe to a focused shortlist. Six stocks are surfaced so users can spend more time investigating a manageable group instead of manually reviewing thousands of charts.

Can I run the NASDAQ scanners myself?

Yes. EdgeBreak provides manual NASDAQ scanners alongside the automated daily process, so users can explore Breakout, Pre-Breakout and Launch Pad results themselves. Explore the NASDAQ Stock Scanner.

What is the Breakout Scanner?

The Breakout Scanner identifies NASDAQ stocks displaying active breakout structures that may warrant further technical and company research.

What is the Pre-Breakout Scanner?

The Pre-Breakout Scanner searches for NASDAQ stocks developing technical structures near resistance, including repeated resistance interaction, higher lows and improving market structure.

What is the Launch Pad Scanner?

The Launch Pad Scanner searches for NASDAQ stocks building organised technical bases and established trading ranges using support, resistance and price structure for further pre-breakout research. Explore Launch Pad.

Market Activity & Evidence
What does EdgeBreak mean by unusual off-exchange activity?

EdgeBreak compares reported off-exchange activity across multiple FINRA reporting venues to identify behaviour that appears unusual relative to a stock's recent history. This adds market activity context behind the technical setup.

Does off-exchange activity confirm institutional buying?

No. FINRA off-exchange data does not identify whether reported activity was buying or selling and does not prove that an institution accumulated a stock. EdgeBreak treats unusual activity as evidence for further investigation, not confirmation.

What is the Pressure Building Index?

The Pressure Building Index is an EdgeBreak research indicator used within the daily process to assess characteristics such as persistence, participation and timing alongside technical structure and market activity.

AI Research
What can EdgeBreak AI Company Research investigate?

EdgeBreak AI Company Research can investigate company information, financial highlights, current market attention, recent news, institutional context and SEC filings. See the AI Stock Research page.

Can EdgeBreak analyse stock charts with AI?

Yes. Users can upload, paste or drag and drop a stock chart for descriptive analysis of visible technical characteristics such as market structure, support, resistance, higher lows, consolidation, volume behaviour and breakout structure.

What is the difference between Guided AI and Conversational AI?

Guided AI provides structured questions for quick explanations and research prompts. Conversational AI lets users ask their own follow-up questions and explore a stock or research topic more naturally.

Can AI-generated EdgeBreak research contain errors?

Yes. AI-generated information can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Important findings should be independently verified using reliable sources, company disclosures and official regulatory filings.

Can beginners use EdgeBreak?
